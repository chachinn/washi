About Washi

和紙

Washi (和紙) is traditional Japanese paper, known for its beauty, texture, and the many ways it can be shaped, layered, and decorated. Washi brings that same idea to your photos, videos, and words—giving you a playful space to arrange little pieces of your life into stories that feel completely your own.
